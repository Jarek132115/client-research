# Source ledger — which discovery channels produce winners

Score each source 0–100 (start 50). Mine high scorers first; promote sources that
surface a published prospect; decay sources that keep producing non-fits.

Format: `source | score | hits | last_useful | good_for | notes`

- Facebook Ad Library | 50 | 0 | — | revenue signal (20+ sustained ads = real budget) | confirms spend, not first-find
- SimilarWeb (free) | 50 | 0 | — | traffic cross-check + category top sites | one of two needed revenue signals
- Trustpilot / category review sites | 50 | 0 | — | review-velocity revenue estimate | sort a category by review count
- Trade/DTC press (Modern Retail, Glossy, Retail Brew) | 50 | 0 | — | brands in growth mode | learn vertical, hunt one tier below
- Instagram / TikTok niche scroll | 50 | 0 | — | follower-count signal | 30k+ then click to site
- Google product search (customer-intent queries) | 50 | 0 | — | finds non-household Shopify stores | skip the giants
- Reddit / niche forums | 50 | 0 | — | enthusiast-vertical brands | good for hobby/automotive/tools
