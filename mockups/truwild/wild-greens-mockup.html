    WILD GREENS FAQ

    How many servings per Jar?

    Each bag contains 30 servings. If you take daily it is a month's supply of greenness!

    Do I need to take everyday?

    To get the most out of WILD GREENS, we recommend making it part of your daily routine. The benefits compound over time once it becomes part of your everyday routine. No one has the perfect diet, so our greens give you peace of mind while covering your nutritional bases, day in and day out.

    How do Truwild superfood greens compare to other greens powder?

    WILD Greens offers a more affordable, great-tasting option with similar superfood ingredients to other brands. Unlike some brands that charge more for added prebiotics and probiotics, TRUWILD provides similar benefits at a lower cost.

    How do I use it?

    Our Greens is a supplement made powerfully simple and convenient.

    While you can take our greens at any time, we recommend drinking it first thing in the morning on an empty stomach for optimal absorption.

    To use, add 8 to 10 ounces of cold water to a glass or a shaker bottle. You can adjust the amount of water based on your taste preference.

    Then, add 1 scoop of powder. Shake or stir until fully blended. Drink and enjoy!

    What country are the ingredients sourced from and are they all organic?

    We source our ingredients from around the globe, but all products are manufactured in a cGMP facility in California, USA.

    Our products are certified USDA Organic, ensuring the highest quality. Enjoy them on-the-go to fill in your nutritional gaps
